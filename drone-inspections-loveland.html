Drone visual inspections.
A closer look at visible conditions.

Angel Eye View LLC provides drone visual inspection imagery in Loveland, CO and Northern Colorado. Document roofs, facades, signs, and hard-to-reach structures with close-up photographs that support maintenance planning, project records, and review by your team.

Document the areas your team needs to see.

Start with a specific question or area of concern. We plan image coverage around the structure, site access, airspace, and the details you want documented.

Roofs & facades

Capture visible roofing, flashing, membranes, shingles, and exterior cladding. Photographs provide a record for review by the contractor or specialist responsible for evaluating the condition.

Signs & structures

Collect views of elevated signs and other hard-to-reach features. Agree on the faces, connections, or areas to photograph before the site visit.

Project & maintenance records

Create dated visual documentation for facility records, construction updates, or insurance claim documentation. The images record visible conditions at the time of capture.

Purposeful image capture

Useful imagery starts with a clear scope.

Share existing photographs, the features of interest, and how your team will review the results. We can discuss overview images for context and closer views for specific details, with coverage subject to safe access and flight conditions.

This service provides visual imagery. It does not replace a structural, engineering, roofing, or other specialist assessment. Photographs cannot establish the condition of concealed components or guarantee that every defect will be visible.
